Privacy Policy

Custom Aim: Aim Trainer — Last updated August 22, 2026

Omar Altamimi ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Custom Aim: Aim Trainer application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Summary

The App has no account system and no server of our own. Most of your data stays on your device. Three things can leave it: the public leaderboard, which you join only if you choose to; an optional iCloud backup of your progress; and data collected by our advertising and purchase partners. Each is described below.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data on your device using standard iOS local storage:

  • Game settings (sensitivity, sound preferences, haptic feedback)
  • Game scores, statistics, and progression data
  • Crosshair configuration and customization preferences
  • Your callsign, country, and profile emblem
  • Onboarding selections

You may delete this data at any time from the App's settings, or by uninstalling the App.

3. Public Leaderboard

The App has an optional public leaderboard. It uses Apple's CloudKit public database in our iCloud container. We operate no server; Apple hosts the data.

You join the leaderboard only after you agree to a consent screen inside the App. If you join, the following is published and is visible to every other player:

  • Your callsign (a display name you choose)
  • Your country flag
  • Your profile emblem and rank badge
  • Your score, and the day of the Daily Gauntlet result
  • A random identifier that keeps your row separate from other rows

Your Apple Account name and your email address are never published. Do not put personal information in your callsign, because a callsign is public.

You may remove your published rows at any time with "Delete Leaderboard Data" in the App's settings. You may also hide or report another player from the leaderboard screen. A report sends us the reported callsign and the reason, so that we can remove content that breaks the rules.

4. iCloud Progress Backup

The App writes a backup of your progress to your own Apple Account, in Apple's CloudKit private database. Only you can read it. It restores your progress when you reinstall the App or move to a new device. We cannot read it. Deleting the App's data also deletes the backup.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Custom Aim Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment card, your bank details, or your Apple Account credentials.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for this App, an anonymous identifier for your installation, and device identifiers such as the vendor identifier and the IP address. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

We also use Apple's AdServices framework to attribute an install to an Apple Search Ads campaign. Apple resolves the attribution token on its own servers, and this needs no tracking permission.

6. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising supports the free part of the App. Custom Aim Pro removes the advertisements.

To serve ads, Google AdMob may collect and process the following data:

  • Device identifiers (Identifier for Advertisers, or IDFA, if you grant permission)
  • IP address (used for approximate location to serve relevant ads)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and conversions)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)

Soon after you first launch the App, you will be asked for consent to tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you will also see a consent form under the GDPR.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — advertising. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management. Privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple CloudKit — the public leaderboard and the private progress backup. Privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy

We use no other analytics platform and no crash reporting tool of our own. Apple sends us crash and performance reports only if you turn on "Share With App Developers" in your device settings, and those reports are aggregated by Apple.

8. Tracking

The App uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework to ask your permission before Google AdMob may access your device's advertising identifier. You may change your choice at any time in Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Only the advertising identifier is used for tracking. Leaderboard data and purchase data are never shared with a data broker and are never used for tracking.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 9+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Ads served to users are configured as non-child-directed. If you believe a child has been served inappropriate content, please contact us.

10. Data Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's built-in security. Leaderboard and backup data are held in Apple's CloudKit and are governed by Apple's security practices. Advertising and purchase data are governed by the security practices of Google and RevenueCat.

11. Your Rights

  • Clear all local game data from the App's settings.
  • Remove your public leaderboard rows with "Delete Leaderboard Data".
  • Hide or report another player from the leaderboard screen.
  • Change your tracking choice in your device settings at any time.
  • Users in the EEA and the UK have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to withdraw consent. Write to the address below to make a request.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be posted on this page with a new revision date. Please review this policy from time to time.
