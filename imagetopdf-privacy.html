Privacy Policy

Image to PDF · Last updated 19 September 2026

The short version: Image to PDF keeps your files on your device. It has no account system, no servers of its own, and no analytics or advertising. Every conversion runs on your phone. We never see your images, your documents, or your PDF files.

Who we are

Image to PDF (the "App") is developed by ItDeveApps ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the App accesses on your device and why. If you have questions, contact us at omar.tamimid1@gmail.com.

What the App accesses

The App turns images, scans, text, and web pages into PDF files. To do that it uses the following, entirely on your device:

  • The images you choose — the App opens the Android photo picker or the files app. It reads only the items you select. It never browses your gallery on its own.
  • The camera — the App starts your device camera app when you take a photo for a page, or when you use the document scanner. The photo goes straight into the PDF you are building.
  • PDF files on your device — the Files screen lists your PDF documents so you can open, merge, compress, protect, or share them. See "All files access" below.
  • The internet — only when you use Web to PDF. The App loads the web address that you type, so that it can print that page to a PDF.
  • Text recognition — the App reads text from an image on your device, through Google ML Kit. The recognition runs offline. No image leaves your phone.
  • Your PDF settings — the App stores your own choices, such as page size, margin, quality, watermark, and header. These stay in the App's private storage.

All files access

The App requests the MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission, which Android shows as "All files access". A PDF tool must find your existing PDF documents wherever you saved them, including folders written by other apps, so that you can merge, compress, split, protect, or rename them.

The App uses this access only to find and open PDF files, and to save the PDF files that it creates. It does not read, upload, index, or transmit any other file. You may refuse the permission. The App still converts images to PDF, but the Files screen then shows only the documents that the App itself created.

Where your data goes

Nowhere off your device, except where you direct it. The App saves the PDF files that it creates to Documents/ImageToPDF on your own device. When you tap Share or Print, Android hands the file to the app that you pick, and that app's own policy applies from that moment.

We operate no servers. We receive none of your images, documents, or PDF files.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software. There is no account and no sign-in, so we never learn your identity. The App does not ask for your name, your email address, your contacts, your location, or your device identifiers.

Web to PDF

When you use Web to PDF, the App opens the address that you type, in the same way that a browser does. That website receives the request, and its owner's own privacy policy applies to it. We do not keep a record of the addresses you enter.

Google Play services

The document scanner and the text recognition come from Google ML Kit. Both run on your device. Google Play services downloads the scanner component after you install the App, and Google may process standard delivery information for that download. Google's handling of that data falls under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your files stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete a PDF inside the App, or through any file manager. You can withdraw All files access in Settings › Apps › Image to PDF › Permissions. When you uninstall the App, Android removes its private storage and your saved settings. The PDF files in Documents/ImageToPDF stay on your device, so that you keep your work.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from them.
